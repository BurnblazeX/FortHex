    FortHex: Guide

    FortHex is a turn-based tactical strategy game played on a hexagonal board. This guide outlines the controls, interface, and rules required to command your army and secure victory.

    1. Basics ►

    Winning the Game: Matches are won by fulfilling one of two conditions:

    • Annihilation: Eliminate all enemy units from the board.
    • Capture the Flag: Move a unit onto the enemy's Base Camp to take their flag, then successfully return that unit to your own Base Camp.

    Controls: Click a unit to view its statistics in the left panel. Valid movement paths will highlight in green. Click a highlighted path to move. To act, click an action button in the right panel (Attack, Fortify, Build Bridge), then click a valid target on the board. Click "End Turn" to pass control.

    Arcade Mode: Played on a compact map without base camps. You have 30 seconds per turn. Starting on turn 2, you are forced to swap one unit's class each turn. The game ends after 10 turns, and the winner is decided by total remaining army health.

    2. The Terrain ►

    Units travel and stand on the roads (edges) connecting the hexagonal tiles. Up to two friendly units can occupy the same road simultaneously. The terrain bordering a road determines its movement point (MP) cost and visibility:

    • Plains: Open ground. Costs 1 MP to traverse. Does not block Line of Sight.
    • Forest: Dense terrain. Costs 2 MP to traverse. Blocks Line of Sight for ranged attacks.
    • Mountain: Tall rock formations. Costs 3 MP to traverse. Blocks Line of Sight for ranged attacks. Cannot be fortified.
    • Water & Beaches: Beaches (edges between land and water) cost 3 MP to traverse. Pure water edges are impassable until a Melee unit converts them into a Bridge. Cannot be fortified.
    3. Your Army ►

    You control four distinct unit classes. Class advantages grant +1 Damage against specific targets, while disadvantages deal -1 Damage.

    • Melee (Sword): HP: 12 | Move: 4 | Attack: 3. Standard frontline infantry. Can consume 1 MP to build a Bridge over a water road. Strong vs Archers. Weak vs Horsemen.
    • Archer (Arrow): HP: 10 | Move: 3 | Attack: 2. Ranged unit. Attacks from a distance without taking counter-attack damage. Gains +2 Attack when fortified. Strong vs Pikemen. Weak vs Melee.
    • Pikeman (Pike): HP: 13 | Move: 3 | Attack: 3. Heavily armored defender. Base defense is higher than other classes. Strong vs Horsemen. Weak vs Archers.
    • Horseman (Horse): HP: 11 | Move: 5 | Attack: 3. Mobile scout. Can attack and use any remaining MP to retreat in the same turn. Cannot fortify. Strong vs Melee. Weak vs Pikemen.
    4. Combat & Abilities ►

    Attacking: Consumes 1 MP.

    Fortifying: Consumes 1 MP. Moves a unit off the road and into the center of an adjacent Plains or Forest tile. Fortified units take reduced damage and exert a Zone of Control (ZoC) that deals 1 damage to any enemy that walks past or starts their turn nearby.

    Unit Synergies: Position your units to utilize combat rules:

    • Combined Arms: If a Melee and Archer occupy the same road, their attacks ignore the defense bonuses of fortified targets.
    • Cavalry Screen: If an enemy Melee attacks your Horseman, a friendly Archer sharing the road will automatically fire a half-damage support shot at the attacker.
    • Spear Wall: Enemy Horsemen attacking an edge adjacent to your fortified Pikeman immediately lose their remaining MP and cannot retreat.
    • Split Damage: If an Archer fires at a road occupied by two enemies, the total damage is split evenly between both targets.
    5. Advanced Mechanics ►

    Supply Lines: You have 10 Supply Points. Fortifying a unit draws a glowing line back to your Base Camp if there is a valid path, reserving points based on distance. Unbroken supply lines allow fortified units to heal +1 HP at the start of your turn (up to +1 Shield over their Max HP) if they have not been attacked that turn. Enemy units standing on the dotted line block this healing.

    Capturing the Flag: The moment a unit picks up the enemy flag, all enemy supply lines are severed. The enemy army cannot heal until they destroy your flag carrier and return the flag to their base.

    Bridge Destruction: Attacking and destroying a built bridge immediately drowns or heavily damages any units standing on it, and severs any supply lines routed across it.

    Reinforcements & Veterancy: Destroyed units enter the Reinforcements queue (bottom left) for 10 turns. When the timer hits 0, a prompt appears. You can either Deploy a new unit at your base, or click the Promote tab to permanently upgrade a surviving unit on the board. Units can reach up to Level 3, gaining Health, Speed, Damage, or Defense. Heavily upgrading one stat will apply a permanent penalty to its paired stat (Health ↔ Speed, Damage ↔ Defense).
